# Биомы v4: исходные запросы

## Финальные поверхности дорог (roads-v4.png)

Use case stylized-concept. Six seamless PIXEL ART ROAD MATERIALS for fantasy tower defense, a precise 3 columns x 2 rows atlas of square cells, NO margins, NO borders, NO lines, NO text. Entire image opaque. Every cell is a top-down seamless uniform road surface, final 256px each represents FOUR game cells across, so keep individual stones SMALL and irregular. Row1col1 forest: warm compact tan earth with sparse embedded tiny rounded grey stones and faint NON-directional worn patches. Row1col2 autumn: slightly ochre compact soil with sparse tiny grey-brown pebbles and a few VERY small rust leaves, low contrast texture. Row1col3 winter: cool blue-grey packed snow paving, small flat stones peeking through a light snow dusting, readable medium blue-grey, NOT pure white. Row2col1 swamp: pale muted grey-beige small dry stone causeway, narrow dark joints with very sparse moss; must contrast with dark olive marsh ground. Row2col2 volcanic: medium warm slate-grey small irregular basalt stones in ash, no lava. Row2col3 dungeon: medium grey-purple small rectangular worn paving stones, hairline dark joints. Style handcrafted fantasy RPG crisp material details, fine 1px clusters at256px, top-down slight upper-left lighting, subdued palette. Crucial NO diagonal stripes, NO wheel tracks/ruts, NO directional brush strokes, NO giant cobblestones, NO paths with edges, NO vignettes, NO illustrations, NO props, NO grass on soil roads. Each material fills its square and repeats seamlessly.

Изображения созданы встроенным imagegen специально для проекта. Исходные PNG сохранены рядом. После генерации выполняются только нарезка, приведение к размерам, подготовка стыков и очистка прозрачности в `tools/prepare_biomes_v4.gd`.
